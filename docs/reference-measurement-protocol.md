# Physical B7K Ultra reference protocol (not a parity certificate)

The existing repository's modeled tone and documented targets cannot establish exact electrical or acoustic equivalence to a commercial pedal without independent measurements of a real, identified unit. No verified historical WAV references are available. **Without real hardware captures, status is UNVERIFIED**, not PASS. A complete capture manifest only earns **REVIEW_REQUIRED** before quantitative DSP analysis.

1. Record the precise manufacturer's model, generation, hardware revision and serial; note firmware/IR if applicable. Do not silently conflate the B7K, B7K Ultra v1/v2, or later variants. Record supply voltage under load and center polarity, warm-up and ambient temperature. Never treat third-party redrawings or marketing specifications as a released manufacturing schematic.
2. Use a calibrated reamp/source fixture with measured source impedance and level. Characterize the pedal input impedance **over frequency**, output impedance/loading, and a representative passive pickup R-L-C source circuit: a low-impedance line-driver transfer function alone cannot establish real pickup/loading parity. Published manual nominal input/output impedance (1 MΩ/1 kΩ) is a starting specification, not a measurement of the tested unit.
3. Record **raw bypass**, clean and overdrive separately; repeat quarter-inch and balanced XLR paths, with the cab simulator explicitly OFF. Record DI transformers/ground lift if engaged, XLR loading, balanced wiring and phantom-power isolation. Keep full-range clean paths distinct from IR/cab simulation, any alternate voicing and audio-interface input impedance.
4. Sweep all eight continuous controls independently (including Master) through minimum, quarter, midpoint, three-quarter and maximum positions with other controls held fixed. Record Attack/Grunt switch labels and the printed Lo-Mid/Hi-Mid frequency selections; the manifest validator requires those positions in every take. This is **metadata completeness only**: it does not assert full combinatorial physical-control coverage or that the printed EQ frequencies equal the measured peaks.
5. For each routing/configuration, record at least two calibrated input levels (e.g. -24 and -12 dBFS, plus an overload sweep for distortion onset). Include logarithmic sweeps, stepped sine, impulses, broadband noise, real bass pickup transients and sustained notes. Save raw, unclipped PCM WAV at one documented sample rate. Measure noise floor, THD+N vs frequency and level, harmonic spectra, intermodulation, dynamic compression, output gain relative to bypass, phase/group delay and frequency response.
6. Do not normalize A/B signal pairs independently: doing so would destroy level and headroom evidence. Calibrate clocks/interface loopback first, estimate and subtract fixture latency, then constrain cross-correlation alignment to a documented latency window; preserve physical pedal phase and group delay. Account for ADC/DAC uncertainty, repeatability across takes and input/output impedance uncertainty. Report confidence intervals and test conditions, not a universal single "accuracy percentage".
7. Preserve **three distinguishable signals per take**: (a) the digital reamp playback stimulus, (b) the *measured dry electrical signal at the loaded pedal input connector* using a sufficiently high-impedance probe/monitor, and (c) the pedal output at the chosen jack. The digital stimulus may legitimately be reused across many knob/switch takes, with a recorded `playback_gain_db` for each; do **not** confuse that open-circuit source signal with the actual impedance-loaded voltage reaching the circuit. Every wet take needs its **own** recorded input monitor and output WAV; do not duplicate either recording across takes. Capture the loaded-input monitor and output with a common ADC clock, document monitor input impedance and the probe/channel gain correction to pedal-input voltage (`dry_monitor.calibration_db_to_pedal_input`). Monitor and output durations must match within 250 ms or 5% (whichever is larger). All WAVs must be nontruncated mono/stereo 16/24/32-bit PCM of at least one second, and the two inputs (digital stimulus and recorded monitor) must not be digitally silent. Constrain paths to the manifest directory; no absolute paths, parent traversal or symlink escapes.
8. Populate `analysis/hardware-captures/manifest.json` using **schema version 2**. For each take, include the path and hexadecimal SHA-256 digest of *all three* WAVs (`stimulus_file`/`stimulus_sha256` for the digital playback source, `dry_input_file`/`dry_input_sha256` for the recorded, loaded-input monitor, `file`/`sha256` for the pedal output), `dry_monitor` measurement and probe metadata, calibrated `input_level_dbfs`, bypass-relative gain, the playback gain, signal route, mode, all eight 0–1 control positions and all four physical selector positions. Calculate hashes **after** recording, before copying/analyzing; compare them again after transfers. On Linux, `sha256sum analysis/hardware-captures/*.wav` prints the digests (on macOS, use `shasum -a 256`). The manifest is not an authenticity signature: someone can fabricate metadata and synthesize matching WAVs; these checks alone establish neither physical origin nor parity. Maintain independent session logs, photos and calibration records.
9. Required capture coverage is one bypass input level and two distinct clean/OD levels on **each** 1/4-inch and XLR output. Additionally, run an independent five-position sweep (0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1) of **each** knob in OD/1/4-inch mode, all other controls at 0.5 and Attack/Grunt at flat, Lo-Mid at 500 Hz and Hi-Mid at 1500 Hz. Run each switch through all of its positions with all knobs at 0.5 and the other switches at the reference settings. This is a **minimum provenance gate**, not comprehensive real-pedal validation; the electrical, nonlinear and other output-path comparisons in steps 1–6 are still required.
10. Run `python3 analysis/validate_hardware_capture.py` or provide `--manifest path/to/manifest.json`. Missing references, mismatched hashes or inadequate independent-control coverage produce `UNVERIFIED` (exit 2). Only complete required metadata and parseable recorded input/output pairs backed by digital playback source evidence produce `REVIEW_REQUIRED` (exit 0), pending actual waveform comparison and independent review. **Neither status is a parity claim. This validator never issues PASS or a percentage of circuit fidelity.** Even synthetically fabricated files that meet every structural check remain only `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, because an SHA-256 digest cannot certify provenance. Run offline tests with `python3 -m unittest discover -s analysis -p 'test_validate_hardware_capture.py'`.

To claim demonstrable parity, compare raw real-pedal captures against plugin output at matched gain/settings with frequency-dependent magnitude/phase, nonlinear level-dependent spectra, noise/dynamic behavior, transient response and control tracking. Publish residual error plots, repeat captures across real pedal units and blind listening tests, with explicit tolerance bands per mode/path. If any required reference is absent, label its grade **UNVERIFIED**.
