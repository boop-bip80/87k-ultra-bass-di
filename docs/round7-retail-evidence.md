# Round 7: retail-versus-clone circuit evidence and physical verification

**Status (2026-11-05):** This repository transcribes and approximates PCB Guitar Mania's **Black Mirror VII** DIY board; it does **not** contain an authenticated factory Darkglass Microtubes B7K Ultra schematic or a verified retail PCB netlist. Agreement with a clone BOM, a fitted transfer function, or the manufacturer's external specifications must not be presented as proof of retail component parity. In this document “verified” means verified *in the cited source*, not verified in a retail specimen.

## Primary-source boundary

1. **Retail product controls and external specifications—high confidence:** Darkglass's [B7K Ultra manual][darkglass-ultra] specifies eight controls (Master, Level, Blend, Drive, Bass, Lo Mid, Hi Mid, Treble), three-position Attack and Grunt controls, 9 V center-negative supply, 120 mA current consumption, nominal 1 MΩ input and 1 kΩ output impedance. The four EQ bands are Bass 100 Hz, Lo Mid selectable 250/500/1000 Hz, Hi Mid selectable 750/1500/3000 Hz, Treble 5 kHz, each ±12 dB. These are external user specifications, **not a retail factory schematic**. The [non-Ultra B7K manual][darkglass-b7k] instead states 500 kΩ input; do not transfer that number to Ultra.
2. **DIY board parts—high confidence for the indicated BOM only:** The maker's [Black Mirror VII 1.6v building document][clone-16] identifies the parts below. The [maker product listing][maker-product] describes its product as based on the B7K Ultra, not as a factory-authenticated recreation.
3. **DIY board topology—medium confidence:** The clone's drawn nets and transcription warrant a hypothesis about that *specific board*. Pin-by-pin circuit identification is only as strong as drawing legibility and revision identity; it must be checked by continuity on an assembled board.
4. **Model outputs—low confidence for physical identity:** The repository's SPICE/analysis parameters are hypotheses or fits. Fitted values are neither stock BOM values nor proof of production Darkglass parts. No retail internals were verified in the reviewed primary material.

## Reproducible physical comparison protocol

1. **Specimen metadata and custody.** Photograph front/rear, serial/lot, switch labels, PCB side(s), IC markings, silkscreen revision, rework and component orientation. Record whether each specimen is factory retail, DIY 1.1v/1.2v/1.6v, or unknown. Do not lift “retail” provenance from visual similarity or a clone seller's marketing.
2. **Unpowered map first.** Disconnect all supply, DI phantom power and audio. Use current-limited DMM continuity/ohms/diode mode for Q1/Q2 terminals, IC3 D1/D2 network, power rails, grounds, switch truth tables, and bypass routes. In-circuit R/C readings can be parallel-loaded: document a lifted end or desoldered independent measurement when values matter. Respect electrostatic and electrolytic polarity precautions.
3. **Powered safety and rail audit.** Only after connector polarity is verified, power at the official nominal 9 V center-negative through a current-limited bench supply with headroom for at least the documented 120 mA draw; start safely below destructive overcurrent, ramp and observe. Scope reference and local supply nodes at every op amp, JFET and CMOS chip, DC and ripple; record current over all control/switch states. Do not assign a ± supply, internal 8.65 V or 4.5 V node by analogy without measurements.
4. **Linearity and topology.** Inject calibrated low-level sine (~20 Hz–20 kHz; e.g. −40 through −10 dBV) through known source impedance. Measure complex transfer magnitude **and phase** at Q1, Q2, IC3, op amp stages and both outputs. Increase input level in controlled steps and record clipping onset, asymmetric wave shape, harmonic spectrum and recoverability; never rely on a single peak gain at one frequency.
5. **Controls and switching.** Run full-state matrices of three-position Attack/Grunt (and clone Ultra-Hi), all EQ selectable centers, potentiometer full/half/end positions, blend, drive, master/level and bypass; measure clean/drive paths independently where possible. Capture THD+N, headroom, input/output impedances with level/frequency controls and actual loads (DI balanced load separately).
6. **Decision gate.** Promote a model claim from hypothesis to clone-as-built only when two independent observations match the named net/component *and* the precise board revision. Promote to **retail physical parity** only when a provenance-verified retail specimen is independently mapped or a factory-origin schematic is authenticated, and a second measurement confirms the relevant behavior. Log counterexamples and uncertainty; *matching frequency response alone is not proof of component topology*.

## Corrections that follow directly from the evidence

- **Retail input impedance:** Ultra's published nominal **1 MΩ** and non-Ultra's **500 kΩ** are distinct product claims. The DIY model's approximate 500 kΩ upper-band result is a **parity discrepancy to test**, not an authoritative assertion about Ultra internals.
- **Q2 identity:** Do not describe the repository's current Q2 model as a second cascaded common-source gain stage. It is wired as a rail-drain / source-to-output JFET with divider-biased gate in that file; verify the physical board before naming its function confidently.
- **Power/headroom:** A model `v9=8.65 V`, `vd=4.5 V` is a scenario, not measured retail dual/charge-pumped rails. Different supplies or bias points drastically change clipping and gain predictions.
- **Component fit:** Adjusted R7/C7/C15/gm values in the simulator are calibration degrees of freedom and must not appear in an as-built BOM without independent measurement.
- **Revision collision:** “1.1v IC6=TL072” is false as an unqualified statement because the bundled dated 2023 1.1v explicitly specifies TL074. Carry source date with revision in every table and figure.
